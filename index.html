    Scoring

    —

    ⏱ The clock is still running while you read this.

    10 each Every word you find Five words in the grid, so 50 points at most.
    1 a sec Every second left on the clock The clock is 5:00, so up to 300 points. You get these only if you find all five words. Four words and the clock runs out pays nothing for speed.
    +5 Streak bonus A flat 5 if you also played in the last two days. It stays 5 — it never grows.

    Example: all five words in 1:12
    50  +  228  +  5  =  283

    Worth knowing

    • Play again today and the new score replaces the old one — even if it is lower. Only what you submit counts.
    • Level on points? The faster time wins. Still level, it goes to milliseconds, then to who used fewer shuffles.
    • Shuffles and power-ups never cost you points.
    • Your 🔥 streak counts days in a row on the calendar. Weekends count. Miss one day and it starts again at 1.
    none The sprint has no points Nothing is added up here. Word points, time points and the streak bonus all belong to the daily puzzle.
    grids You are ranked on puzzles cleared How many full grids you finished in five minutes. That one number is your place on the board.
    5:00 One clock for everybody Only runs of five minutes or less show on the week and all-time boards. The sprint used to be ten minutes, and those runs would clear roughly twice as many grids.

    If two people are level

    • Today: most puzzles cleared, then most words found, then the faster time.
    • Week and all time: most puzzles in total, then the best single run, then most words in total.
    • A real tie shares the place on the sprint board.
    • Power-ups are shown next to your name, but they never cost you anything.
